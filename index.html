Integer Module 1

Adding Integers

Adding integers combines positive (gain/rightward) and negative (loss/leftward) values.

Rule 1: Same Signs

Add absolute values and keep the common sign. Example: (-5) + (-3) = -8

Rule 2: Different Signs

Subtract smaller absolute value from larger, keeping the sign of the larger. Example: (-9) + (+4) = -5

Zero Pairs Concept

Positive (+1) and Negative (-1) cancel each other out to make zero.

(-4) + (+6)
4 Negative Counters cancel 4 Positive Counters
+2 Remaining
Integer Module 2

Subtracting Integers

Subtraction is equivalent to adding the additive inverse (opposite). Use the KCC rule!

KCC Rule (Keep - Change - Change)

Keep 1st number, Change minus to plus, Change sign of 2nd number.

(-6) - (-10) → (-6) + (+10) = +4
Examples at a Glance
(+7) - (+12) → (+7) + (-12) = -5
(-3) - (+8) → (-3) + (-8) = -11
Integer Module 3

Multiplying Integers

SAME SIGNS → POSITIVE
(+) × (+) = +
(-) × (-) = +
DIFFERENT → NEGATIVE
(+) × (-) = -
(-) × (+) = -
(-6) × (-7)+42
(-8) × (+5)-40
Integer Module 4

Dividing Integers

Sign rules for division are identical to multiplication! Note: Division by 0 is UNDEFINED.

(-36) ÷ (-6) = +6
(+45) ÷ (-9) = -5
Zero in Division

0 ÷ (-7) = 0 (Allowed)
(-15) ÷ 0 = UNDEFINED (Cannot divide by 0!)

Fraction Module 1

Adding & Subtracting Fractions

Fractions must have a Common Denominator (LCM) before you can add or subtract numerators!

Step 1: Like Denominators

Keep the denominator, add/subtract numerators: 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7

Step 2: Unlike Denominators

Find LCM of denominators, convert equivalent fractions, then calculate.

1/3 + 1/4 → 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12
Worked Example
Calculate: 5/6 - 1/4
LCM of 6 and 4 is 12
5/6 = 10/12 | 1/4 = 3/12
10/12 - 3/12 = 7/12
Fraction Module 2

Multiplying & Dividing Fractions

Multiplication

Multiply numerator by numerator, denominator by denominator! Direct calculation.

(2/3) × (4/5) = (2×4) / (3×5) = 8/15
Division (Keep-Change-Flip)

Keep 1st fraction, Change ÷ to ×, Flip 2nd fraction (reciprocal).

(3/4) ÷ (2/5) → (3/4) × (5/2) = 15/8 = 1 7/8
Remember: Always Simplify!

Divide numerator and denominator by their Greatest Common Factor (GCF).

12/18 → Divide top and bottom by 6 → 2/3
Fraction Module 3

Improper Fractions & Mixed Numbers

Mixed to Improper

Multiply whole number by denominator, add numerator.

2 3/5 → ((2×5) + 3)/5 = 13/5
Improper to Mixed

Divide numerator by denominator. Quotient = Whole, Remainder = Numerator.

17/5 → 17 ÷ 5 = 3 remainder 2 → 3 2/5
Grade 7 Pro Tip

Always convert mixed numbers into improper fractions BEFORE multiplying or dividing!

Decimal Module 1

Operating with Decimals

Addition & Subtraction

Line up the decimal points vertically! Fill missing places with zeroes.

12.40 + 3.85 = 16.25 | 7.50 - 2.18 = 5.32
Multiplication

Multiply normally, then count total decimal digits in both factors to place the point.

0.3 (1 decimal) × 0.04 (2 decimals) = 0.012 (3 decimals)
Decimal Division Rule

Shift the decimal point right in both divisor and dividend until divisor is a whole number.

Calculate: 4.8 ÷ 0.12
Shift 2 places right → 480 ÷ 12
= 40
Decimal Module 2

Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

Rational numbers can be represented interchangeably in fraction, decimal, or percentage form.

1/2 = 0.5 50%
1/4 = 0.25 25%
3/4 = 0.75 75%
1/5 = 0.2 20%
Quick Conversion Formulas
Fraction → Decimal: Divide numerator by denominator (e.g. 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375)
Decimal → Percent: Multiply by 100 (e.g. 0.375 × 100 = 37.5%)

Integers Problem 1

Ocean Submarine Exploration

A research submarine is submerged at -150 m. It descends another 75 m, then rises 90 m. What is its current depth?

Equation: (-150) + (-75) + 90

→ Answer: -135 meters

Fractions Problem 2

Baking Flour Calculation

Siti has 2 1/2 kg of flour. She uses 3/4 kg to bake a cake, and then buys another 1 1/4 kg. How much flour does she have now?

Equation: 5/2 - 3/4 + 5/4 = 10/4 - 3/4 + 5/4 = 12/4

→ Answer: 3 kg

Decimals Problem 3

Supermarket Bill & Change

Budi buys apples for $4.75 and milk for $3.40. He pays with a $10.00 bill. How much change should he receive?

Total Spent: $4.75 + $3.40 = $8.15

Change: $10.00 - $8.15

→ Answer: $1.85

Integers Problem 4

Jayawijaya Mountain Temperature

At dawn, the temperature was -5°C. By noon, it rose by 11°C, and at night dropped by 8°C. What is the final temperature?

Equation: (-5) + 11 - 8

→ Answer: -2°C

Fractions Problem 5

Classroom Pizza Party

Anisa eats 2/8 of a large pizza and Anton eats 3/8. What fraction of the pizza is remaining for their friends?

Eaten: 2/8 + 3/8 = 5/8

Remaining: 1 - 5/8 = 8/8 - 5/8

→ Answer: 3/8 of the pizza

Decimals Problem 6

Athletic Running Laps

Each lap around the school field is 0.4 km. If Rian completes 6.5 laps, what total distance in kilometers did he run?

Equation: 0.4 × 6.5

→ Answer: 2.6 km

Quick Rules Cheatsheet

Integers

Same signs: Add & keep sign.
Diff signs: Subtract & keep sign of larger abs value.
(-) × (-) = (+), (-) ÷ (-) = (+)

Fractions

Add/Sub: Must find Common Denominator (LCM).
Mul: Top × Top, Bottom × Bottom.
Div: Keep, Change (×), Flip reciprocal.

Decimals

Add/Sub: Line up decimal points vertically.
Mul: Count total decimal places.
Div: Shift decimal points to make divisor a whole number.
